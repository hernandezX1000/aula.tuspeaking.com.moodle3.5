Class eleven - AI in Daily Life – From Smart Speakers to Self-Driving Dreams
Objectives
  • Vocabulary & Comprehension: Students will be able to identify and use key vocabulary related to Artificial Intelligence (e.g., algorithm, automation, chatbot) to describe how AI is applied in daily life.
  • Grammar & Communication: Students will be able to use future forms (will and going to) to make predictions and talk about plans connected with AI technologies.
  • Critical Thinking & Expression: Students will be able to express their opinions and participate in discussions about the advantages and challenges of AI in society, using appropriate language structures and examples.
