Plan lectivo – Nivel A1–A2
Inglés general · tuSpeaking
El presente plan lectivo establece los contenidos formativos correspondientes a los niveles A1–A2 del Marco Común Europeo de Referencia para las Lenguas (MCER), estructurados en unidades temáticas que integran competencias léxicas, gramaticales y comunicativas.
TEMA 1: PERSONAL IDENTITY

Competencia léxica: Personal information; countries & nationalities; numbers; dates; jobs; basic descriptors (age, appearance); greetings & introductions.

Competencia gramatical: Verb “to be” (affirmative/negative/questions); subject pronouns; possessive adjectives; articles (a/an/the); there is/are (intro); simple present (intro); question words (what/where/who).

TEMA 2: HOME & ENVIRONMENT

Competencia léxica: Rooms & furniture; household objects; places in town; directions (basic); neighbourhood; weather (basic).

Competencia gramatical: There is/are; prepositions of place (in/on/under/next to); demonstratives (this/that/these/those); can/can’t (ability); imperative (basic instructions).

TEMA 3: DAILY LIFE & ROUTINES

Competencia léxica: Daily routines; time & schedules; household chores; hobbies (basic); frequency expressions.

Competencia gramatical: Present simple (affirmative/negative/questions); adverbs of frequency; like/love/hate + -ing (intro); telling the time; short answers (Yes, I do / No, I don’t).

TEMA 4: FOOD & RESTAURANTS

Competencia léxica: Food & drinks; meals; ordering in a restaurant/café; prices; preferences.

Competencia gramatical: Countable/uncountable nouns; some/any; much/many/a lot of; would like; questions with “how much/how many”; present simple for habits.

TEMA 5: LEISURE & FREE TIME

Competencia léxica: Leisure activities; sports; entertainment; invitations & arrangements; days/months/seasons.

Competencia gramatical: Present continuous (in progress / near future intro); making suggestions (let’s / shall we…?); prepositions of time (at/on/in); future “going to” (intro).

TEMA 6: SHOPPING & CONSUMPTION

Competencia léxica: Shops & services; clothes & sizes; money & prices; basic complaints/returns.

Competencia gramatical: Comparative adjectives (intro); demonstratives; can/could (requests intro); this/that/these/those; past simple (intro: was/were).

TEMA 7: TRAVEL & ACCOMMODATION

Competencia léxica: Accommodation; Transport network; Travel.

Competencia gramatical: Preguntas y respuestas con interrogativos (WHAT, WHICH, WHOSE, HOW); modal auxiliaries (CAN, MAY, SHOULD, MUST, MIGHT, HAVE TO); interrogative sentences.

TEMA 8: SOCIAL RELATIONS

Competencia léxica: Family, social and religious acts & celebration; Family relations; Social relations.

Competencia gramatical: Adjectives; many/much/a lot; possessives; countable & uncountable nouns; quantifiers; like & dislike; adverbs of frequency.

TEMA 9: EDUCATION

Competencia léxica: Educational institutions; Education teaching & learning; Educational system; The teaching process.

Competencia gramatical: Possibility & permission (may/might); comparison of adjectives; passive voice (present simple & simple past); determiners (another/other); indefinite determiners (a few, a little, much, many, a lot of, most, any, enough, some, no, each, every, either, etc.); past simple (-ed e irregulares).

TEMA 10: FOOD (Basics, places & payments)

Competencia léxica: Places, people & activities; Clothes, footwear & complementary wear; Food; Payments.

Competencia gramatical: Formas impersonales del verbo to be en pasado; There + was/were; It + to be; past simple; be (negativo e interrogativo).

TEMA 11: FOOD (Recipes, diet & nutrition)

Competencia léxica: Food recipes; food and preferences; alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks; food, diet and nutrition.

Competencia gramatical: Uses of “Do” (auxiliary / full verb); uses of “Be” (auxiliary, passive, full verb, There + be); uses of “Have” (auxiliary, full verb, have/have got).

TEMA 12: GOODS & SERVICES

Competencia léxica: Post/Mail services; Financial services; Health services; Protection & security services.

Competencia gramatical: Present perfect tense; formas impersonales del verbo be en present perfect.

TEMA 13: MASS MEDIA & COMMUNICATION

Competencia léxica: Information and communication; written communication; telephone; news and journalism.
